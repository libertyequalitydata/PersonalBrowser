# Code of Conduct

## Principles

- Treat contributors and users with respect.
- Discuss code and behavior without personal attacks or harassment.
- Protect private reports and personal information.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, or disclosure of private information are not accepted. Maintainers may remove contributions or participation that violate these rules. Send private conduct reports to `hello@prifina.com` with the subject `Personal Browser conduct report`. Include only information relevant to the report and state whether the reporter's identity may be shared during review. Do not disclose conduct reports in public issues. This document creates no guaranteed moderation or response SLA.
